# Resource button logos

- `arxiv.svg`: official small logomark from [arXiv logo assets](https://info.arxiv.org/brand/logos.html), [SVG download](https://cornell.box.com/v/arxiv-logomark-small-svg). The original SVG is preserved; CSS displays it in white on the dark button.
- `github-white.svg`: official white Invertocat from the [GitHub Brand Toolkit](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo), [logo archive](https://brand.github.com/GitHub_Logos.zip).

These marks identify the destinations of links to the paper and code repository. They remain the trademarks of their respective owners.

## Varest identity

`varest-mark.svg` is the original project symbol: a geometric V assembled from unequal regions. A large navy region balances finer slate-blue subdivisions, reflecting semantic variable-resolution tokenization. Flat fills and open gaps keep the mark legible at small sizes and in print.

`varest-logo.svg` pairs the symbol with a restrained serif wordmark. `../favicon.svg` uses the same symbol on a white background. The university marks remain separate from the project identity.
